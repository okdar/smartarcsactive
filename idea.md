* Analog watch face for Garmin watches with round screens
* Designed to provide an overview of daily activity (steps and floors), body battery, and stress level
* Arc-shaped indicators around the watch face for notification count, Bluetooth status, Do Not Disturb mode, alarm count, and battery level
* Additional indicators for sunrise/sunset and civil dawn/dusk times
* Optional second hand
* Power saver mode to conserve battery: during a defined time window, only the clock hands are drawn and refreshed every 5, 10, or 15 minutes; the full watch face is restored when the watch becomes active
* Lost and found feature: if the watch loses Bluetooth connectivity for a specified period, the owner's contact information is displayed instead of the watch face
* Optimized for the Vívoactive 4 with its 260×260 screen resolution
* Minimizes CPU utilization, memory allocation, and battery consumption for optimal performance
